## Content Quality Issues
1. **Hero paragraph** (~55 words, one sentence) is generic, calls the firm a "company", and is wrapped in markup copied from a chat interface (`font-claude-response`, `standard-markdown`, `data-is-streaming`). Rewrite: *"Enterline & Partners is a U.S. immigration law firm with offices in Ho Chi Minh City and Manila, founded and managed by licensed U.S. attorneys. We handle K-1 fiancé(e), CR-1/IR-1 spousal, EB-5 investor and employment-based cases for clients in Vietnam and the Philippines."* (confirm every claim first).
2. **FAQ 04 (cost)** answers nothing quotable. Add ranges or a fee structure, e.g. "Attorney fees for a K-1 case start at [X]; government and consular fees are separate ([Y])" with a date.
3. **FAQ numbering** ("05." twice) and question links that are not headings — make each question an H3 so it maps to a query.
4. **Sentence length** in FAQ 02 (30+ words) and FAQ 01 — split into 15–20 word sentences.
5. **Testimonial carousel** content is undated, unattributed to a platform and not answer-shaped; keep it but don't rely on it for citability.
6. **Page debris:** hundreds of empty `jso-cursor-trail-shape` divs saved inside two text blocks; remove.
7. **Footer copyright** "2018 – 2025" is stale.

## AI Content Concerns
- The hero copy was demonstrably pasted from an AI chat interface (the class names above). That is acceptable under the rubric if reviewed by the attorneys, but nothing on the page says so.
- Several FAQ answers follow a uniform pattern with summarizing closers ("This accountability matters — especially for…"). That pattern is common in AI-assisted drafting; it's fine if attorney-reviewed, but the answers contain little that is specific to Vietnam or the Philippines beyond naming the countries.
- Recommendation: add "Reviewed by [attorney name], [date]" to FAQ and guide content, and inject firm-specific details (consulate procedures in HCMC/Manila, local document requirements).

## Citability Assessment
### Most Citable Passages
1. **FAQ 05 — processing times** (bulleted, per visa category, with numbers): easy to extract; add a date and source to make it quotable with confidence.
2. **FAQ 01 — "Yes. Enterline & Partners is founded and managed by licensed U.S. immigration attorneys…"**: direct answer first, names both offices.
3. **FAQ 03 — pathways for Vietnamese and Filipino nationals** (five-item list): matches how users phrase the query.
4. **FAQ 02 — attorney vs. immigration consultant**: a clear, opinionated comparison; shorten the sentences.
5. **K-1 tile — "The K-1 visa is for the fiancé(e) of a U.S. citizen. After entering the U.S. and marrying within 90 days, you can apply for permanent resident status."**: self-contained and accurate; the EB-5 tile is similar.

- **Hero paragraph** — generic, single long sentence.
- **FAQ 04 (cost)** — no data.

- **News list** — titles and dates only; no summaries on the homepage.

## Improvement Recommendations
### Quick Wins
1. Replace FAQ 04 with real fee guidance; fix numbering; make questions H3s; add "Last reviewed" date and update the footer year.
2. Rewrite the hero paragraph (above) and strip the pasted chat/cursor-trail markup.
3. Add privacy policy and terms links, and an attorney-advertising / "not legal advice" notice, to the footer. Ask counsel whether bar advertising rules require disclaimers on testimonials and past-result claims.
4. Expand each attorney card with bar admission(s), years admitted, memberships (e.g., AILA if applicable) and a link to a full bio.
5. Add sources (USCIS, State Department Visa Bulletin) next to every number.

### Content Gaps
Topics not visible in the homepage navigation — check whether they already exist before creating them:
- CR-1 vs. IR-1: who qualifies, what changes after two years of marriage
- Government + consular + attorney fee breakdown by visa type
- Step-by-step process pages (petition → NVC → interview → entry) with Vietnam and Philippines specifics
- Document guides: Vietnamese civil documents and police certificates; Filipino civil documents and pre-departure requirements for fiancé(e)s

- EB-5 source-of-funds and cross-border transfer documentation for Vietnamese investors

- Publish 3–5 anonymized, consented case summaries: situation, obstacle, action, timeline, outcome.
- Add press/speaking/publication evidence and third-party profiles (Google Business Profile, Avvo/Justia, AILA) and link them in `sameAs`.
- Move testimonials to a dated, attributed format, ideally sourced from a third-party review platform.
- Unify the brand name and the office addresses across page, schema and social profiles.
