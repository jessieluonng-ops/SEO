# Implementation Roadmap (12 months)

## Phase 1: Foundation (weeks 1–4)
- [ ] Discovery data (see SEO-STRATEGY §7); live SERP benchmark; fill COMPETITOR-ANALYSIS
- [ ] Confirm advertising/compliance rules with the firm's lawyers
- [ ] Hosting on fast infra with HTTPS; target LCP<2.5s, INP<200ms, CLS<0.1 on mobile
- [ ] GA4 + Search Console + Bing Webmaster; conversion events (WhatsApp/Zalo/phone click, form)
- [ ] **Google Business Profile**: exact legal name, correct primary category (Immigration attorney / Law firm), real address, hours, services, video verification prepared
- [ ] NAP consistent everywhere; core citations (Google, Bing, Apple Maps, legal/expat directories)
- [ ] Core pages: Home, About, Team (lawyer profiles), Contact, top 3 service pages (work permit, TRC, business visa)
- [ ] LegalService/Organization/Person schema; robots.txt allows major search + AI crawlers per firm policy
- [ ] EN/VI structure + hreflang set up

## Phase 2: Expansion (weeks 5–12)
- [ ] Remaining service pages (800+ words each, unique, FAQ, timeline, document list)
- [ ] 8–12 informational articles (costs, timelines, rejection reasons, comparisons like "business visa vs work permit")
- [ ] Vietnamese versions of top pages (native-reviewed)
- [ ] Review generation system: ask every satisfied client, link to GBP, target 15 reviews by month 3; reply to all
- [ ] Internal linking pass; breadcrumbs; sitemap submitted
- [ ] First "Immigration updates" post; start `Last reviewed` stamps

## Phase 3: Scale (weeks 13–24)
- [ ] Nationality/location pages where genuinely differentiated
- [ ] 2–4 new articles/month + quarterly refresh of all service pages
- [ ] Outreach: chambers of commerce, expat groups, legal directories, guest expert commentary, HARO-style press
- [ ] GEO: quotable answer blocks, FAQ, get listed on "best immigration lawyers in Vietnam" roundups; monitor citations in ChatGPT/Perplexity/AI Overviews
- [ ] CWV and template optimisation; monthly `/seo audit` style check
- [ ] Rank tracking on the basket (EN/VI, per city, mobile)

## Phase 4: Authority (months 7–12)
- [ ] Original content: annual "Immigration in Vietnam" guide, processing-time survey, anonymised case studies
- [ ] Media/podcast/webinar appearances by named lawyers
- [ ] Video content (YouTube explainers) embedded on service pages
- [ ] Expand languages (KR/JP/ZH) if client data supports it
- [ ] Review and prune underperforming pages; consolidate cannibalisation

## Success criteria per phase
| Phase | Exit criteria |
|---|---|
| 1 | Site indexed, GBP verified, tracking live, 3 service pages published, CWV Good |
| 2 | All core service pages live, 10+ articles, 15 reviews, first basket keywords in top 20 |
| 3 | 8–12 basket keywords in top 10, GBP top 5, steady enquiries from organic |
| 4 | 15–20 basket in top 10, several at #1–3, GBP top 3, ≥70 reviews |

## Resources
1 licensed lawyer (≈4h/week review + bylines), 1 writer/editor (EN+VI), 1 dev/SEO (part-time), tool budget for rank tracking/keyword data. Dependencies: lawyer review time is the usual bottleneck.
