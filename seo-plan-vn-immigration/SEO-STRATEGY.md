# SEO Strategy: Immigration Law Practice, Vietnam

_Mode: new-site planning (no URL supplied). Template: `local-service` + YMYL legal adjustments. No live SERP/keyword data was available in this session, so every number below is a planning assumption to validate (see "Data to collect first")._

## 1. Reality check on "rank #1"
- No one can guarantee #1. Realistic goal: **#1–3 in the Google Local Pack and top-5 organic for a defined basket of 15–25 money keywords**, in 9–12 months, in the city where the firm has a real office.
- "Top 1" must be defined per keyword + city + language + device. Track a basket, not a single term.
- Legal is **YMYL**: Google weights E-E-A-T, real lawyer authorship and trust signals heavily. Thin/AI-only content will not win.

## 4. Strategy pillars
1. **Local dominance**: Google Business Profile, reviews, NAP, city pages (only where genuinely serving).
2. **Service pages that convert**: one deep page per visa/permit type (800+ words, unique, with process, documents, timeline, fees range, FAQ, lawyer byline).
3. **Trust / E-E-A-T**: named licensed lawyers, bar membership, case experience (anonymised), office photos, editorial and review dates, sources to primary legislation/government portals.
4. **Freshness**: immigration rules in Vietnam change often; a dated "Immigration updates" hub and visible `Last reviewed` stamps are a ranking and trust edge.
5. **AI-search readiness**: quotable answers, FAQ blocks, consistent entity data, presence on "best immigration lawyers/consultants in Vietnam" lists and expat forums.
6. **Links & mentions**: expat communities, chambers of commerce (EuroCham, AmCham, etc.), legal directories, press. Earned, not bought.

## 5. Compliance guardrails (legal marketing)
- Vietnamese lawyer advertising is regulated (Law on Lawyers and Bar Federation rules). **Have the firm confirm what claims are permitted** before publishing: avoid "guaranteed approval", "100% success", "best lawyer" superlatives.
- Include disclaimer: general information, not legal advice; no lawyer-client relationship until engagement.
- Verify every legal statement against current law/regulations (Law on Entry, Exit, Transit and Residence of Foreigners and amendments, Labour Code, work-permit decrees). Date-stamp pages.

## 8. Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Rule changes make content stale | Quarterly legal review cadence, `dateModified` + visible review date |
| Established competitors with old domains | Win on specificity (visa-type × nationality × city), reviews, fresh content, and speed of response |
| GBP suspension (virtual office/keywords in name) | Real staffed address, exact legal name, video verification ready |
| Thin machine-translated Vietnamese | Native lawyer/editor review; proper hreflang |
| Non-compliant ad claims | Compliance checklist before publish |
