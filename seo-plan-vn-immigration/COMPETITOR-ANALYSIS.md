# Competitor Analysis Framework: Vietnam Immigration Law

_No live SERP access in this session, so **no named competitor metrics are asserted**. Below is the archetype map and the exact procedure to fill it with real data. Do this in week 1._

## Competitor archetypes to expect
| Archetype | Typical strengths | Typical weaknesses / your opening |
|---|---|---|
| Established law firms (multi-practice, international) | Domain age, backlinks, brand | Immigration is a side page; generic content, slow to update |
| Visa/work-permit agencies ("consultants", not always lawyers) | Heavy blog volume, low prices, many landing pages | Weak E-E-A-T, no named lawyers, thin/duplicated pages. You beat them on credentials |
| Expat-service companies (relocation, HR/PEO) | Broad expat audience, forums/links | Not legal advice, limited depth per visa type |
| Aggregators/directories & expat forums | Rank for informational queries | Outdated info; opportunity to be cited/listed there |
| Government portals | Authoritative | Dense, hard to use; your job is clarity + checklists |

## How to build the top-5 table (procedure)
1. Google the basket (EN + VI), incognito, VN location, desktop + mobile. Record map-pack 3 and organic top 10 per query.
2. Count frequency of appearance → the 5 most-recurring domains are the real competitors.
3. For each, record: domain rating/authority, indexed pages, # of visa-type pages, avg word count, schema types (view source/Rich Results Test), lawyer bylines, review count/rating on GBP, languages, last-updated visibility, page speed (PSI).
4. Keyword gap: run domain intersection (DataForSEO/Ahrefs) → keywords 2+ competitors rank for, you don't.

## Differentiation levers (regardless of who the top 5 are)
- Named, licensed lawyer bylines + reviewer credits on every page.
- Nationality-specific pages (e.g. "Work permit for US citizens in Vietnam") where requirements or docs genuinely differ.
- Transparent process + realistic timeline + fee ranges (competitors often hide these).
- Rule-change alerts with dates.
- Real client reviews mentioning visa type and city.
